Experience

AI & Computer Vision Engineer - Ideofuzion

May 2023 - Present

Islamabad, Pakistan. Joined as Sr. Game Developer and moved into applied AI.

  • Sentry Vision, on-premise AI surveillance. Designed and built a multi-camera theft-detection system running 8-10 RTSP cameras in real time on a $200-class used GPU, with no cloud inference and no video leaving the site.
  • Architected a two-stage alert pipeline: sub-second pose and behaviour heuristics raise the candidate, a local vision-language model confirms it seconds later. Chosen after benchmarking showed a 0.87 inference/s VLM ceiling made per-frame model calls impossible at any camera count.
  • Cut VLM verification cost ~97% (785 triggers down to 22-69 verifications, measured over a 10-minute 9-camera soak) by grouping detections into episodes with a sliding rejoin window and scene-cut detection.
  • Built a GPU benchmark harness ramping synthetic camera fleets against decode, latency and queue-depth gates. It disproved the team's CPU-bound assumption: decode held 60/60 fps at 30 cameras while GPU pose saturated at ~26, which rewrote the hardware costing model and caught three deployment bugs before install.
  • Eliminated hallucinated weapon detections by re-asking the model a binary question on a zoomed crop and cross-checking against pose evidence: open-ended prompts invented knives from phones, binary framing did not. Also root-caused a missed sub-3s alert target (median 6.75s) to a serial verify queue and whole-file JSON persistence, not inference.
  • Immersive AR, live stadium player tracking. Built and deployed the server-side CV pipeline: two 4K RTSP feeds through YOLO detection on TensorRT FP16, BoTSORT tracking with appearance re-ID, homography pitch calibration, team classification and PaddleOCR jersey identification, on a single NVIDIA L2 at roughly half a GPU per camera.
  • Held per-frame CV latency to ~23 ms and end-to-end CV-to-AR to 88-153 ms against a 500 ms p95 target, by running jersey OCR asynchronously in two GPU worker processes so identification never gates frame emission.
  • Raised identification reliability with confidence voting, pose-based torso cropping and x2 super-resolution ahead of OCR, and cut identity-swap artefacts with a track-ID stabiliser using speed-scaled re-association, team-colour confirmation and a 12 m/s velocity cap to reject teleports.
  • Wrote the capability assessment that stopped the organisation over-claiming: replaced an unmeasured "98% accuracy" headline with the measured pair (96-98% merged, 80-92% single-camera), killed an inconsistent sub-200 ms glass-to-glass figure, and flagged a "60,000 concurrent users" claim as infrastructure never load-tested.
